## Layout
- Left sidebar: the workspace name at the top (click it to switch workspaces), then Search, Home, Inbox, then your pages grouped under Teamspaces, Shared and Private. Settings, Templates and Trash are in the sidebar too; their exact position moves between versions.
- A page: breadcrumbs at the top left. At the top right: Share, the comments icon, the star (favourite) and the three-dot menu with page options.
- Everything on a page is a block: a paragraph, heading, to-do, image, toggle, or a database.
- Hover to the left of any block to see + (add a block below) and the six-dot handle (drag, or click for the block menu).

## Where things are
- New page: the New page button at the top of the sidebar.
- Add any kind of block: type / on an empty line and pick from the list (heading, to-do, table, toggle, callout, code, image).
- Change a block's type: six-dot handle > Turn into.
- Make a database: type /table, /board, /calendar, /list, /gallery or /timeline.
- Add another view of a database: the + beside the view tabs at its top.
- Filter and sort a database: the Filter and Sort buttons at its top right.
- Add or hide columns (properties): the database's settings or three-dot menu at its top right > Properties.
- Share or publish to the web: Share at the top right.
- Full width, small text, font, lock: the page's three-dot menu.
- Export to PDF, HTML or Markdown: the page's three-dot menu > Export.
- Earlier versions of a page: the page's three-dot menu > Version history.
- Bring back a deleted page: Trash in the sidebar.
- Members, plans, import and connections: Settings.

## Gotchas
- A database row is itself a page. Click Open on a row to see its content.
- A linked view of a database shows the same rows. Deleting a row there deletes it everywhere.
- Filters on a shared view apply for everyone who uses that view.
- A page inherits who can see it from its parent. Moving it can change who has access.
- Typing / inside existing text opens the block list; press Esc to type a plain slash.

## Shortcuts
- Search or jump to a page: Cmd+K (also Cmd+P). New page: Cmd+N.
- Markdown as you type: # heading, - bullet, [] to-do, > toggle, ``` code.
- Link to a page: type @ or [[ and its name.
- Comment: Shift+Cmd+M. Duplicate a block: Cmd+D.
- Show or hide the sidebar: Cmd+Backslash. Dark mode: Shift+Cmd+L.
