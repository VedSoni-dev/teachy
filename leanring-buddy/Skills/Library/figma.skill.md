## Layout
- Left sidebar: the Figma logo (main menu) and file name at the top, then the File and Assets tabs, the Pages list, and the Layers list.
- Canvas in the middle.
- Toolbar at the bottom centre: Move, Frame, shapes, Pen, Text, Comment, Actions. On the same bar is the switch between Design and Dev Mode. (Before UI3 the toolbar was along the top.)
- Right sidebar: Share and the Present (play) button at the top, then the Design and Prototype tabs. Design shows properties of the selection: position, layout, Auto layout, appearance, fill, stroke, effects, and Export at the bottom.
- With nothing selected, the right sidebar shows the page colour, local variables and local styles.

## Where things are
- Every menu command: the Figma logo at the top left, or the Actions button in the toolbar and type its name.
- Make a frame: F, then drag or pick a device size in the right sidebar.
- Auto layout: select layers, then Shift+A.
- Make a component: select, then Option+Cmd+K. Find components: Assets tab.
- Export an image: select a layer, scroll to Export in the right sidebar, click +, pick the format, then Export.
- Link screens: Prototype tab, then drag the round handle from a layer to the target frame.
- Play the prototype: the Present button at the top right.
- Version history: main menu > File > Show version history.
- Invite people or copy a link: Share at the top right.
- Turn on a team library: Assets tab > the book icon.
- Developer view with measurements and code: Dev Mode (Shift+D).

## Gotchas
- A group only bundles layers. A frame has its own size, fill, clipping and layout. Auto layout, constraints and prototyping want frames.
- Clicking selects the top-level frame first. Double-click to go deeper, or Cmd+click to select the exact layer.
- In the browser, the menu is behind the Figma logo, not the browser's own menu bar.
- Changes save by themselves. There is no Save button.
- A missing font shows a warning icon next to the text; that text cannot be edited until the font is replaced.

## Shortcuts
- Move: V. Frame: F. Rectangle: R. Text: T. Pen: P. Comment: C. Pan: hold Space.
- Zoom to fit: Shift+1. Zoom to selection: Shift+2.
- Group: Cmd+G. Duplicate: Cmd+D. Rename: Cmd+R.
- Show or hide the interface: Cmd+Backslash. Copy as PNG: Shift+Cmd+C.
