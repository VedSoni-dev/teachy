## Layout
- Title bar: AutoSave, the Quick Access Toolbar (save, undo, redo) and a search box.
- The ribbon, with tabs: Home, Insert, Draw, Page Layout, Formulas, Data, Review, View. Extra tabs appear when a table, chart or pivot table is selected (Table, Chart Design, PivotTable Analyze).
- Under the ribbon: the Name Box at the left (shows the cell, such as B4) and the formula bar (fx).
- The grid. Sheet tabs at the bottom left, with + to add one.
- Status bar at the bottom: the sum, average and count of the selected cells, and the zoom slider at the right.
- The Mac menu bar at the top of the screen (File, Edit, View, Insert, Format, Tools, Data, Window, Help) holds some commands that are not on the ribbon.

## Where things are
- Add up a column: Home > AutoSum.
- Filter and sort: Data > Filter, Data > Sort.
- Keep headings in view: View > Freeze Panes.
- Turn a range into a table: Insert > Table.
- Pivot table: Insert > PivotTable. Chart: Insert > Recommended Charts.
- Colour cells by rule: Home > Conditional Formatting.
- Number, date and currency formats: the Number menu on Home, or Format > Cells.
- Wrap text and merge cells: Home > Wrap Text, Home > Merge & Center.
- Remove duplicates, split a column, dropdown lists: Data > Remove Duplicates, Text to Columns, Data Validation.
- Find a function: Formulas > Insert Function.
- Lock a sheet: Review > Protect Sheet.
- Save as CSV or PDF: File > Save As > File Format.
- Settings: Excel > Preferences. Add the Developer tab under Ribbon & Toolbar.

## Gotchas
- A cell full of ##### is only too narrow. Widen the column.
- On Mac laptops the F keys need Fn held down unless that is changed in System Settings > Keyboard.
- Saving as CSV keeps only the active sheet and drops formatting and formulas.
- AutoSave works only for files stored in OneDrive or SharePoint.
- A number aligned to the left of its cell is stored as text and will not add up.

## Shortcuts
- Edit the cell: F2 or Ctrl+U. New line inside a cell: Option+Return.
- Absolute reference ($A$1) while typing a formula: Cmd+T or F4.
- AutoSum: Shift+Cmd+T. Fill down: Cmd+D. Fill right: Cmd+R.
- Format Cells: Cmd+1. Insert a table: Cmd+T (when not editing a formula).
- Filter on and off: Shift+Cmd+F. Paste Special: Ctrl+Cmd+V.
- Select a column: Ctrl+Space. Select a row: Shift+Space.
- Jump to the edge of the data: Cmd+Arrow. Show formulas: Ctrl+Backtick.
