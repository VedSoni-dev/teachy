## Layout
- Toolbar: View, Zoom, Add Slide, Play, then Table, Chart, Text, Shape, Media, Comment, then Collaborate or Share, and at the right Format, Animate, Document.
- Slide navigator on the left (thumbnails). The slide in the middle.
- Format, Animate and Document each open a sidebar on the right. What Format shows depends on what is selected: text, a shape, an image, or the slide itself when nothing is selected.

## Where things are
- Change the theme: Document > Document tab > Change Theme.
- Slide size (16:9, 4:3, custom): Document > Document tab > Slide Size.
- Change a slide's layout: click the slide background, then Format > Slide Layout.
- Slide transition: select the slide in the navigator, then Animate > Add an Effect.
- Animate an object: select it, then Animate > Build In, Action or Build Out. The Build Order button at the bottom of that sidebar sets timing.
- Magic Move: duplicate the slide, move things on the copy, then give the first slide the Magic Move transition.
- Presenter notes: View > Show Presenter Notes.
- Edit the layouts behind every slide: View > Edit Slide Layouts.
- Crop a picture: Format > Image > Edit Mask. Remove a background: Format > Image > Instant Alpha or Remove Background.
- Rehearse: Play > Rehearse Slideshow. Record narration: Play > Record Slideshow.
- Export to PowerPoint, PDF, a movie or images: File > Export To.

## Gotchas
- With nothing selected, Format shows slide options, not text options. Click the text first.
- Builds play in Build Order, not top to bottom on the slide.
- A skipped slide looks like a thin line in the navigator and does not play.
- Fonts missing on another Mac are replaced, which shifts layouts. Export a PDF when the look must hold.
- Play starts at the selected slide, not the first.

## Shortcuts
- New slide: Shift+Cmd+N. Duplicate: Cmd+D.
- Play: Option+Cmd+P. Stop: Esc.
- Presenter notes: Shift+Cmd+P. Rehearse: Option+Cmd+R.
- Group: Option+Cmd+G. Skip slide: Shift+Cmd+H.
