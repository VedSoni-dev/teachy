## Layout
- Top bar: the main menu (three lines) at the left, the search box with a filter-options icon at its right end, then Help (?), Settings (gear), Google apps and your account picture.
- Left: the Compose button, then Inbox, Starred, Snoozed, Sent, Drafts and More (which reveals All Mail, Spam, Trash, Categories, Manage labels, Create new label). Your labels are listed below.
- The inbox may have tabs across the top: Primary, Promotions, Social, Updates.
- Tick a message and a row of buttons appears above the list: Archive, Report spam, Delete, Mark as unread, Snooze, Add to Tasks, Move to, Labels, and a three-dot More menu.
- Compose opens a small window at the bottom right, with Send, formatting, attach, link, emoji, Drive, photo, confidential mode, signature, and a three-dot menu.

## Where things are
- Every setting: gear > See all settings. Tabs there: General, Labels, Inbox, Accounts and Import, Filters and Blocked Addresses, Forwarding and POP/IMAP, Advanced, and more.
- Signature: Settings > General > Signature.
- Out-of-office reply: Settings > General > Vacation responder, near the bottom.
- Longer time to undo a send: Settings > General > Undo Send (up to 30 seconds).
- Rules that label, archive or forward: Settings > Filters and Blocked Addresses > Create a new filter, or the filter-options icon in the search box.
- Forward all mail: Settings > Forwarding and POP/IMAP.
- Send later: the arrow beside Send > Schedule send.
- Templates: switch on in Settings > Advanced, then in Compose: three dots > Templates.
- Block a sender: open their message > three dots at its top right > Block.
- Select every message, not just this page: tick the box above the list, then click "Select all conversations".

## Gotchas
- Most settings pages need Save Changes at the very bottom, or nothing is kept.
- Archive only removes the Inbox label. The message stays in All Mail and in search.
- Replies are grouped into one conversation. Turn that off in Settings > General > Conversation view.
- Labels are not folders: one message can carry several, and deleting a label does not delete mail.
- After Send, Archive or Delete, an Undo link shows at the bottom left for a few seconds.

## Shortcuts
- Send: Cmd+Return. Add Cc: Shift+Cmd+C. Insert a link: Cmd+K.
- Single-key shortcuts work only after Settings > General > Keyboard shortcuts is turned on: c compose, / search, e archive, r reply, a reply all, f forward, # delete, s star, u back to the list.
- Show the shortcut list: ? (Shift+Slash).
