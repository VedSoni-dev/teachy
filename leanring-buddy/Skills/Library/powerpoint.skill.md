## Layout
- Title bar: AutoSave, the Quick Access Toolbar (save, undo, redo) and a search box.
- The ribbon, with tabs: Home, Insert, Draw, Design, Transitions, Animations, Slide Show, Record, Review, View. Extra tabs appear for a selected shape or picture (Shape Format, Picture Format).
- Slide thumbnails down the left. The slide in the middle.
- Under the slide: the Notes pane (click Notes in the status bar to show it).
- Status bar at the bottom: slide number at the left; Notes, Comments, the view buttons (Normal, Slide Sorter, Slide Show) and the zoom slider at the right.

## Where things are
- New slide and its layout: Home > New Slide, Home > Layout.
- Theme, colours and background: Design. Slide size (16:9 or 4:3): Design > Slide Size.
- Ready-made layouts for the current slide: Design > Designer.
- Pictures, video, audio, shapes, charts, text boxes: Insert.
- Movement between slides: Transitions. Movement of objects on a slide: Animations, with the Animation Pane for order and timing.
- Line things up: select them, then Shape Format > Align (also under Home > Arrange).
- Change fonts and logos on every slide at once: View > Slide Master.
- Start presenting: Slide Show > Play from Start, or Play from Current Slide.
- See notes and the next slide while presenting: Slide Show > Presenter View.
- Record narration and timings: Record, or Slide Show > Record Slide Show.
- Slide numbers and footers: Insert > Header & Footer.
- Save as PDF or video: File > Export.
- Make the file smaller: File > Compress Pictures.

## Gotchas
- Text typed into a plain text box does not appear in the outline and ignores the slide's layout. Use the layout's placeholders where possible.
- Reset on Home puts a slide's placeholders back to the layout's positions and fonts.
- Fonts that are missing on another computer are swapped, which shifts text. Use common fonts or export a PDF.
- Animations play in the order shown in the Animation Pane, not top to bottom on the slide.
- A hidden slide (crossed-out number in the thumbnails) is skipped when presenting.
- AutoSave works only for files stored in OneDrive or SharePoint.

## Shortcuts
- New slide: Shift+Cmd+N. Duplicate the selected object: Cmd+D.
- Play from the start: Shift+Cmd+Return. From the current slide: Cmd+Return.
- Presenter View: Option+Return. End the show: Esc.
- While presenting: B blacks the screen, W whitens it.
- Group: Option+Cmd+G. Ungroup: Option+Shift+Cmd+G.
- Bold: Cmd+B. Insert a link: Cmd+K.
