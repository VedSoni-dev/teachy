## Layout
- A bar across the top: the LinkedIn logo and the search box at the left, then Home, My Network, Jobs, Messaging, Notifications, and Me (your photo with a small arrow). Further right is For Business. In a narrow window these show as icons only.
- Me opens a small menu: View Profile, then Account (Settings & Privacy, Help, Language), then Manage (Posts & Activity, Job Posting Account), then Sign Out.
- The Settings page has its own menu down the left: Account preferences, Sign in & security, Visibility, Data privacy, Advertising data, Notifications. The chosen section's options are listed on the right; clicking one opens it.
- Your profile page has an intro card at the top with a pencil icon, and buttons under it such as Open to and Add profile section.

## Where things are
- Change your email: Me > Settings & Privacy > Sign in & security > Email addresses > Add email address. Verify it from the email LinkedIn sends, then come back and choose Make primary.
- Change your password: Sign in & security > Change password.
- Two-step verification: Sign in & security > Two-step verification.
- Name, headline, location: Me > View Profile > the pencil on the intro card.
- Custom profile address: Me > View Profile > Public profile & URL, at the right.
- Browse profiles privately: Settings > Visibility > Profile viewing options.
- Who can see your connections or email: Settings > Visibility.
- Download your data: Settings > Data privacy > Get a copy of your data.
- Email and push notifications: Settings > Notifications.
- Dark mode and language: Settings > Account preferences > Display.
- Premium and billing: Settings > Account preferences > Subscriptions & payments.
- Close or pause the account: Settings > Account preferences > Account management.

## Gotchas
- LinkedIn asks for your password, or a code, before it lets you add an email or change security settings.
- A new email cannot be made primary until it is verified. The old one can be removed only after another is primary.
- Settings opens on Account preferences, not on Sign in & security. Pick the section on the left first.
- Profile edits can be announced to your network. The switch is in Settings > Visibility > Share profile updates with your network.
- Signed-out pages and the mobile site are laid out differently from this.

## Shortcuts
- LinkedIn has no general keyboard shortcuts. Use the browser's: Cmd+L for the address field, Cmd+F to find on the page.
- In Messaging, Return sends by default; the three-dot menu in the message box can switch that to Click Send.
