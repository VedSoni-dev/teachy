## Layout
- Toolbar: the sidebar button, Back and Forward, the Smart Search field (address bar) in the middle, then Share, New Tab (+) and Tab Overview at the right. The toolbar can be customised, so buttons may differ.
- Tabs sit under the toolbar (Separate layout) or inside it (Compact layout). Switch in Safari > Settings > Tabs.
- The sidebar holds Tab Groups, Bookmarks, Reading List and Shared with You.
- View > Show Favorites Bar adds a row of bookmarks under the address field.

## Where things are
- Settings: Safari > Settings (Cmd+Comma).
- Clear history and website data: History > Clear History.
- Private window: File > New Private Window (Shift+Cmd+N).
- Extensions: Safari > Settings > Extensions.
- Camera, microphone, location, pop-ups and auto-play per site: Safari > Settings > Websites, or Safari > Settings for [this site].
- Developer tools: Safari > Settings > Advanced > Show features for web developers, then Develop > Show Web Inspector (Option+Cmd+I).
- Empty the cache: Develop > Empty Caches (Option+Cmd+E).
- Download folder: Safari > Settings > General > File download location.
- Search engine: Safari > Settings > Search.
- Profiles (work, personal): Safari > Settings > Profiles.
- Reader: View > Show Reader (Shift+Cmd+R).
- Saved passwords: Safari > Settings > Passwords (opens the Passwords app on macOS 15 and later).

## Gotchas
- The Develop menu is hidden until it is switched on in Settings > Advanced. Before Safari 17 the checkbox read "Show Develop menu in menu bar".
- A normal reload can serve cached files. Hold Option and choose View > Reload Page From Origin (Option+Cmd+R).
- The address field shows only the site name by default. Turn on Settings > Advanced > Show full website address to see the whole URL.
- Extensions come from the App Store and must be ticked in Settings > Extensions before they run.

## Shortcuts
- Address field: Cmd+L. New tab: Cmd+T. Close tab: Cmd+W. Reopen closed tab: Shift+Cmd+T.
- Next and previous tab: Ctrl+Tab and Ctrl+Shift+Tab.
- Bookmark: Cmd+D. Find on page: Cmd+F. Zoom: Cmd+Plus and Cmd+Minus.
- Show all tabs: Shift+Cmd+Backslash.
