## Layout
- Sidebar on the left: Favorites, then each account's mailboxes (Inbox, Drafts, Sent, Junk, Trash, Archive), Smart Mailboxes and On My Mac.
- Message list in the middle, the open message on the right.
- Toolbar above: Get Mail, New Message, Archive, Delete, Junk, Reply, Reply All, Forward, Flag, Mute, Move, and Search at the right.
- macOS 15.4 and later can sort the inbox into Primary, Transactions, Updates and Promotions, shown as buttons above the list.

## Where things are
- Add an email account: Mail > Add Account.
- Signature: Mail > Settings > Signatures.
- Rules that file or flag mail automatically: Mail > Settings > Rules.
- New mailbox (folder): Mailbox > New Mailbox.
- Attach a file: the paperclip in the message window (Shift+Cmd+A).
- Send later: the small arrow next to the Send button.
- Undo a send: Undo Send at the bottom of the sidebar, for 10 seconds by default. Change the delay in Mail > Settings > Composing.
- Block a sender: click the arrow next to their name in the message header > Block Contact.
- Mark everything read: Mailbox > Mark All Messages as Read.
- Remind Me: Control-click a message.
- Fonts and what the list shows: Mail > Settings > Fonts & Colors, and Viewing.

## Gotchas
- Out-of-office replies for iCloud and Gmail accounts are set on the provider's website, not in Mail.
- Deleting from a Gmail account may archive instead, depending on Mail > Settings > Accounts > Mailbox Behaviors.
- A message stuck in the Outbox usually means the outgoing server sign-in failed. Check Window > Connection Doctor.
- Search looks in the selected mailbox first. Click All Mailboxes under the search field to widen it.

## Shortcuts
- New message: Cmd+N. Send: Shift+Cmd+D. Get mail: Shift+Cmd+N.
- Reply: Cmd+R. Reply All: Shift+Cmd+R. Forward: Shift+Cmd+F.
- Mark unread: Shift+Cmd+U. Flag: Shift+Cmd+L. Archive: Ctrl+Cmd+A.
- Search: Option+Cmd+F.
