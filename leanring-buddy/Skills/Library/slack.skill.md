## Layout
- A narrow rail at the far left: the workspace icon at the top, then Home, DMs, Activity, Later and More. Your profile picture is at the bottom of the rail.
- The sidebar beside it (in Home): the workspace name at the top, then Threads, Huddles, Drafts & sent, then the Channels, Direct messages and Apps lists.
- The search bar runs across the very top of the window.
- The conversation is in the middle: the channel name at the top (click it for details, members and settings), tabs under it (Messages first, then others such as a canvas, Pins and Files), and the huddle (headphones) button at the top right.
- The message box at the bottom has + (attach, shortcuts), formatting, emoji, mention, record clip, and the Send button with a small arrow for scheduling.
- Threads and profiles open in a panel on the right.

## Where things are
- Preferences: your profile picture > Preferences (Cmd+Comma). Notifications, themes, sidebar and language are there.
- Set a status or pause notifications: your profile picture.
- Notifications for one channel: Control-click the channel > Change notifications. Mute it from the same menu.
- Jump to any channel or person: Cmd+K.
- Start or join a channel: Add channels at the bottom of the Channels list.
- Invite someone to the workspace: workspace name > Invite people.
- Send later: the arrow next to Send > Schedule for later.
- Be reminded of a message: hover it > three dots > Remind me about this.
- Save a message: hover it > the bookmark icon. Find it under Later.
- Edit or delete your message: hover it > three dots.
- Start a huddle: the headphones button in the channel header.
- Workspace admin settings: workspace name > Tools & settings.

## Gotchas
- Return sends. Shift+Return makes a new line. This can be swapped in Preferences > Advanced.
- @channel and @here notify everyone in the channel. Use them sparingly.
- A reply in a thread does not show in the channel unless "Also send to channel" is ticked.
- Deleting a message cannot be undone. Edited messages are marked (edited).
- Leaving a private channel means someone has to invite you back.

## Shortcuts
- Jump to: Cmd+K. Search: Cmd+G. All unreads: Shift+Cmd+A. Threads: Shift+Cmd+T.
- Edit your last message: Up Arrow in an empty message box.
- Mark a message unread: Option+click it. Upload a file: Cmd+U.
- Back and forward in history: Cmd+[ and Cmd+].
- Toggle a huddle: Shift+Cmd+H. All shortcuts: Cmd+Slash.
