## Layout
- Left ribbon (thin bar on the far left): icons for the command palette, graph view, daily note, templates and others from plugins.
- Left sidebar: File explorer, Search and Bookmarks as tabs at its top. The vault name at the bottom left switches or manages vaults; the gear next to it opens Settings.
- Middle: notes in tabs. Each tab has a reading and editing toggle at the top right (the book or pencil icon) and a more-options menu.
- Right sidebar (toggle on the right edge): Backlinks, Outgoing links, Tags and Outline.

## Where things are
- New note: Cmd+N, or the new note icon at the top of File explorer.
- Link to another note: type [[ and pick it. Embed one: ![[ ].
- Find any command: Cmd+P (command palette).
- Jump to a note by name: Cmd+O (quick switcher).
- Community plugins: Settings > Community plugins > turn off Restricted mode, then Browse.
- Themes and fonts: Settings > Appearance.
- Sync between devices: Settings > Sync (paid) or keep the vault in iCloud Drive.

## Gotchas
- Notes are ordinary .md files; renaming one in Finder breaks links that Obsidian would have updated if you renamed it inside Obsidian.
- Live preview hides Markdown until the cursor is on that line; switch to Source mode in the options menu to see everything.
- Plugins can change shortcuts and add ribbon icons, so a vault may not look like a fresh one.

## Shortcuts
- Command palette: Cmd+P. Quick switcher: Cmd+O. Search all notes: Cmd+Shift+F.
- Toggle reading view: Cmd+E.
