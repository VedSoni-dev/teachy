## Layout
- Home window: tabs along the top (Home, Team Chat, Meetings, Contacts and others, depending on your account). Home has New meeting, Join, Schedule and Share screen. Your avatar at the top right opens settings and status.
- Meeting window: the video in the middle and the toolbar along the bottom: Mute and Start video on the left, then Participants, Chat, Share, Record, Reactions and More, and the red Leave or End on the right. The toolbar hides until you move the mouse.

## Where things are
- Pick a microphone or camera: the small arrow next to Mute or Start video.
- Invite people from inside a meeting: Participants > Invite, then Copy invite link.
- Share one window or the whole screen: Share, pick it, then Share. Tick Share sound to include audio.
- Record: Record (or More > Record). Local recordings are saved to the Zoom folder in Documents once the meeting ends.
- Change your name in the meeting: Participants, hover your name > More > Rename.
- Background and filters: the arrow next to Start video > Choose background.
- Breakout rooms: Breakout rooms in the toolbar or under More, host only.
- Settings (audio test, video, recording location): avatar > Settings in the home window.

## Gotchas
- Screen sharing needs Screen Recording permission for Zoom in System Settings > Privacy & Security; Zoom asks to restart after it's granted.
- Hosts see End; others only see Leave. A host leaving must assign a new host or end it for all.
- Toolbar buttons that don't fit move under More.

## Shortcuts
- Mute: Cmd+Shift+A. Video: Cmd+Shift+V. Share: Cmd+Shift+S.
- Hold Space to talk while muted. Leave: Cmd+W.
