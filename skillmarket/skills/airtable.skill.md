## Layout
- Home: your workspaces and bases. Create, top left.
- Inside a base: tables are tabs along the top. Below them, the toolbar for the current view: the views menu at the left, then Hide fields, Filter, Group, Sort, Color, row height and Share view.
- The views sidebar (Grid, Form, Calendar, Gallery, Kanban and others) opens from the views menu.
- Along the very top: Data, Automations and Interfaces, then Share at the top right.

## Where things are
- Add a field (column): the + at the right end of the field headers.
- Change a field's type or formula: click the field's header arrow > Edit field.
- Expand a record to see every field: the expand arrows next to the row number, or Space with the row selected.
- Make a form people fill in: views > Form. Share its link from Share form.
- Link two tables: add a field of type Link to another record.
- Automations (send an email when a record changes, and so on): Automations at the top of the base.
- Import a CSV: the + next to the table tabs > Import data.
- Undo a change: Cmd+Z, or a record's history in the expanded record.

## Gotchas
- Filters, sorts and hidden fields belong to the view, so everyone using that view sees them. Make a personal view to change things for just yourself.
- Deleting a field deletes its data in every view.
- Some field types and record limits depend on the plan.

## Shortcuts
- Expand record: Space. Search: Cmd+F.
- New record: Shift+Enter. Undo: Cmd+Z.
