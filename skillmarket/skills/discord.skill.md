## Layout
- Far left: the server list. Direct Messages is the Discord logo at the very top; the + at the bottom adds or joins a server.
- Next to it: the channel list of the open server (text channels with #, voice channels with a speaker). The server name at the top opens a menu with Invite People, Server Settings, Notification Settings and more.
- Bottom of the channel list: your avatar, mute, deafen and the gear for User Settings.
- Middle: messages and the message box. Right: the member list (toggle with the people icon at the top right).

## Where things are
- Invite someone: server name > Invite People, then copy the link.
- Start a DM or group DM: Discord logo > the + next to Direct Messages.
- Join a voice channel: click it. Leave: the phone-with-x button above your avatar.
- Share your screen: in a voice channel, the Screen button in the voice panel at the bottom left.
- Mute a channel or server: right-click it > Mute.
- Pinned messages and threads: icons at the top right of a channel.
- Change your nickname for one server: server name > Edit Per-server Profile.
- Search messages: the search box at the top right.

## Gotchas
- User Settings (the gear by your avatar) apply everywhere; Server Settings (from the server name) only exist if you have permission.
- Some channels are hidden until you pick roles, often in a channel called roles or Channels & Roles at the top of the list.
- Shift+Enter makes a new line; Enter sends.

## Shortcuts
- Quick switcher: Cmd+K. Search: Cmd+F.
- Mute: Cmd+Shift+M. Deafen: Cmd+Shift+D.
- Mark server read: Escape on the server, Shift+Escape for all.
