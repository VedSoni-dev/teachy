## Layout
- Home: the search bar for templates at the top, Create a design at the top right, your recent designs below.
- In the editor: the object panel down the far left (Design, Elements, Text, Brand, Uploads, Tools, Projects, Apps). Choosing one opens its panel next to it.
- The page is in the middle; the toolbar for whatever is selected sits just above it (font, colour, effects, animate, position).
- Top bar: File (resize, version history, download), undo and redo, the design's name, Share at the top right.
- Bottom right: zoom and the page grid view.

## Where things are
- Change the size of a design: Resize (top left, next to File). Making a copy in the new size is the safe choice.
- Add text: Text panel, or press T.
- Replace a photo in a template: drag a new one from Uploads or Elements onto it.
- Remove a background: select the image > Edit > BG Remover (a paid feature on some plans).
- Line things up: select > Position in the toolbar.
- Download as PNG, PDF or MP4: Share > Download, then pick the file type.
- Present slides: Present at the top right, or Share > Present.
- Invite people to edit: Share, add their email, and set Can edit.

## Gotchas
- Elements and templates with a crown are for paid plans; they show a watermark until you pay or swap them out.
- Clicking a grouped item selects the group first. Click again to select one part.
- Changes save on their own. Version history is under File.

## Shortcuts
- Text: T. Rectangle: R. Line: L. Circle: C.
- Group: Cmd+G. Duplicate: Cmd+D. Lock: Option+Shift+L.
- Zoom to fit: Option+Cmd+0.
