Teachy is now an assistant, not a course app

Courses are gone. Teachy is now a Mac assistant you talk to: ask about anything on your screen and it helps you right there, or does the work for you.

  • Voice answers about your screenHold control + option and ask. Teachy looks at your screen and starts talking back as soon as it has the first sentence.
  • Pointing and drawingIt moves its cursor to the button you need, and can sketch, lay out cards, or build a 3D model over your screen to explain things.
  • DictationHold fn + control in any text field and talk; Teachy types it in. Double-tap to keep it listening hands-free.
  • MemoryWhat Teachy remembers about you lives in two plain Markdown files on your Mac, PROFILE.md and VOLATILE.md. Edit them and it picks up the change.
  • Agent jobs with a permission gateHand Teachy a job and it plans, works through it, and reports back. Anything that touches your Mac asks first: allow once, for this job, always, or not now.
  • DriveTeachy can operate an app for you by reading its buttons and menus, not just guessing from pixels. Press Escape, click, or move your mouse and it stops right away. Password and card fields are always left to you.
